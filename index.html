More than just chess.

Josephite Chess Club is a community of students brought together by strategy, competition and the love of Chess.

Josephite Chess Club is one of the oldest and renowned club of St. Joseph Higher Secondery School Situated in Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh. Students Here Strive For Exellence and Members Of the club Strive for Checkmates.
